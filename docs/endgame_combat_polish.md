# Endgame combat cues and earned progression

Fractures and all five God Hunts now use named hazard warnings, explicit boss openings, and live modifier guidance. The expedition board still owns entry, attempts, room advancement and rewards.

## Hunt counterplay

Protected bosses point to gold-marked weak points or interactive markers. A marked target receives a gold outline and **BREAK TO EXPOSE**; a required bell, broken term or plinth receives **USE TO EXPOSE**. These markers follow the actual current shield requirements, including the switch from carrying a term to depositing it.

**VULNERABLE** shows the current timed opening while the shield can still return. Further weak-point breaks or feeding pauses can update that timer. Destroying the last required weak point or depositing both terms instead shows **SHIELD BROKEN**, without a misleading closing timer. **RECOVERING** appears only when the boss has no pending action, defense, live attack or visible source warning. Its duration also stops at the next possible arena-pattern attack. Exposure is not a promise that a boss cannot attack.

Faults, oathless slams and the reversed Nhal rhythm display stable attack ordinals such as **2/3**. Simultaneous procession, bell-pulse and absent-space lines instead identify their spatial **LANE**. Hazards retain their authored names through the active **DANGER** stage. Persistent elite scars never display their long implementation lifetime as a countdown.

## Fracture rules

The compact combat panel reports the active Sigil's current rules:

- Fevered Cinders counts burning enemies actually eligible for faster movement.
- Healing Echoes counts live healing echoes and directs the player away from the healing position.
- Elite Scars counts remaining scars and marks their dangerous ground.
- Divine Surge shows the active fragment-power window or time until the next surge.
- Unequal Shelter shows the actual damage bonus and exposed resistance family/value.
- Inheritance previews the final-room consequence, then names the live original boss's inherited traits and their counters. Lightning links, Null fields and Rift strikes also receive named world warnings.

## Presentation and compatibility

Required actor, mechanism and warning labels share bounded placement around the objective, rules panel, focus card and minimap. Shifted labels retain a leader to their original world position. Gold priority rings, text and hazard geometry remain available with Reduced Effects. Positioning is cosmetic: it changes no hit shape, navigation, damage or attack time.

Cues are derived from existing Core state. Additive view properties omit inactive defaults; persisted snapshots, catalog identities, combat timing and permanent rewards are unchanged. The final visible zero-tick attack frame still blocks a recovery cue. Copied bosses cannot become the objective after the original dies, and dead actors cannot remain priority targets.

## Earned progression measurement

`aw balance endgame <fresh-output-directory> [seed-count 1..3]` measures a fresh campaign followed by Fracture tiers 1–3 across all five disciplines. It uses earned passive points and owned equipment under the existing campaign upgrade policy. It grants no artificial levels, health, equipment or victories. Reports separate combat ticks from menu operations, retain failures and finite retries, and preserve command replay and save/restore evidence.

See [native and mechanics verification](endgame_polish_verification.md) and [earned progression results](endgame_progression_verification.md). Automated completion does not establish human difficulty or enjoyment; a hands-on playtest remains the next qualitative check.
