# Complete build loadouts

Open **C → Character → Build loadouts**, or **Gear → Build loadouts**, to inspect eight named builds per character. Visit Mara in Greyhaven while alive to save, rename, delete or apply one. Equipment-only presets remain available at Torren.

A complete loadout remembers:

- The current discipline and all equipment slots, including empty slots and both hands/rings.
- Installed fragments and selected Manifestations.
- Skill mutations and passive ranks in Offense, Defense and Resource.

Saving and renaming are free. Replacing or deleting an existing loadout requires confirmation. Deleting one removes only the saved arrangement. Item upgrades stay attached to their original instances; loadouts never duplicate items, grant fragments or restore old item statistics.

## Preview and switch

Select a saved build to compare its equipment, anatomy, mutations and passives with your current choices. The screen shows exact material costs and any unmet requirements before **Review and apply…** opens a confirmation.

Switching uses the existing build rules. Removing allocated passive ranks charges the ordinary passive-refund fee once; adding only available points is free. Fragment installation/removal and mutation selection retain their existing zero-material cost. Equipment changes require Torren's rescue, even when applying at Mara. Saved mutations require mastery, fragments require ownership, and new Manifestation choices require their resonance threshold. An already selected suppressed Manifestation can remain selected.

A saved build does not retrain your discipline. Retrain separately before using a loadout from a different discipline. Missing, destroyed or incompatible items block application with a reason; re-save the slot after repairing the build. Existing favorite/lock controls, saved-outfit warnings and unused-item filters recognize gear referenced by complete loadouts.

The transaction applies every part together and spends the displayed materials once, or leaves the whole character unchanged. Confirmation is canceled when the character, build, selection or service context changes. Loadouts cannot bypass campaign, defeat or active-expedition restrictions.

## Compare in training

**Practice current build** approaches Greyhaven's training ground. Apply a selected saved loadout first if you want to test it. Training reports name matching loadouts and retain the previous same-character attempt across build changes. Compare damage, resource usage and defensive results under the same training mode and conditions. Practice grants no progression and returns your character unchanged; loading or changing characters clears the transient comparison.

## Persistence

Loadouts belong to the character's normal save and replay history. They store bounded choices and owned-item references, with validation on restoration. The optional field is omitted when no loadouts exist, preserving older unused-save serialization and hashes. A stale loadout remains available for inspection, repair or deletion.
