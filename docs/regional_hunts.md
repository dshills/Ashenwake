# Greyhaven regional hunts

Use **Regional hunts** on the right side of the HUD to inspect the three contracts. To depart, approach the physical hunt board in Greyhaven, click it or press **F**, select an unlocked contract and confirm **Accept & depart**. Completing Acts 1, 2 and 3 unlocks their respective contracts.

| Contract | Unlock | Encounter | Bounty |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The Cinder Pallbearer | Complete Act 1 | An armored mourner and its ranged choir; interrupt the violet dirge and avoid sonic lanes. | Pyrebound Treads + 12 materials |
| Briarwidow of the Maw | Complete Act 2 | An antlered predator and bloom carriers; dodge charges and leave poison blooms. | Widow's Last Echo + 18 materials |
| Kilnmaw the Unquenched | Complete Act 3 | A furnace sentinel, heat tender and emberlings; interrupt the bellows, leave conveyor lanes and retreat from death explosions. | Furnaceheart Cinch + 24 materials |

Every hunt begins with three marked pieces of evidence. Click a marker to walk over and inspect it, or approach and press **F**. The board's **Follow next clue** button also approaches the next marker. The third clue reveals the encounter. **J** or **B** opens your current contract while a hunt is unresolved.

Defeat the entire pack, choose **Return to Greyhaven**, approach the hunt board and **Claim bounty**. The legendary item and materials enter permanent inventory together, once per completed contract. There are no mid-hunt loot drops or progression rewards. You can repeat a contract with a new run identity after claiming its bounty. A free inventory slot is required when departing.

Hunts preserve the campaign route. Save/load remembers tracking, combat, victory awaiting claim and failed attempts. While a contract is unresolved, finish or explicitly abandon it before changing builds, entering training or starting another journey. Abandonment forfeits the bounty. Defeat opens the damage recap; returning to Greyhaven resolves the failed attempt and allows another contract.

The board pauses play and supports the minimum 780-pixel window. Locked contracts conceal future quarry details. World markers and named quarry labels use the existing stylized regional art and combat archetypes. Hunt arenas currently have no local-map overlay; evidence markers and objective guidance provide navigation.

The persisted ledger is bounded to 1,000 admitted regional hunts per character. This includes abandoned attempts. See [verification](regional_hunts_verification.md) for test and review evidence.
