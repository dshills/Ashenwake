# First steps

Open **Journey → First steps** to revisit the optional opening guide. It pauses the world while you read, and groups your first build, available Greyhaven services and basic controls in one scrollable screen.

During ordinary opening play, contextual hints explain mouse movement, approach-to-interact, dodging, interrupting casts and collecting loot. They use your current key bindings. **Got it** dismisses that lesson; **Hide hints** disables automatic hints. The First steps screen remains available, including its toggle to enable hints again. Hints stay hidden behind menus, during training and in optional encounter arenas.

The build guide follows earned progress: inspect and equip Pyrebound Treads, inspect Heart of Serath with Mara, then practice your current build at the training ground. Equipment and anatomy completion marks reflect the current build; practice is remembered after you actually deal training damage. Training remains available before those upgrades so you can practice any earned build.

Available service cards reflect the character's real unlocks. Their actions open the existing equipment, anatomy, stash or hunt previews, or walk to the selected specialist or training ground when in Greyhaven. Away from the hub, a visit action opens Journey and explains the return requirement. These controls do not spend materials, choose a graft, start a hunt or skip a travel confirmation.

Preferences and completed lessons belong to the character's save filename, in an optional `.guidance.json` sidecar with a validated backup. They do not change gameplay saves, replay hashes or reward ownership. Unreadable, foreign or future-version files are preserved; the guide remains usable for the current session. Automatic basics are limited to Act I, and service notices can continue as later specialists become available. Hidden rooms and their rewards are not listed.
