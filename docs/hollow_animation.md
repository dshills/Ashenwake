# Hollow Night creature animation

Act V's creatures now use distinct motions:

- Doubled shadows have offset, unsettling body movement, sharp attack poses and a collapsing death.
- Breach echoes use unnatural casting preparation and recovery, recoil and a dissolving collapse.
- Breach Heart changes its posture with the actual shield state, follows through on resolved attacks and falls when defeated.

The separate breach monument preserves immediate phase, living-channel and causal-warning signals. Secondary motion responds to shield and channel changes, and a staged final containment settles its rings, shutters and surviving wound. The ending does not depict a restored god or a completely erased breach.

These changes are cosmetic: actor roots, collision, combat timing, rewards, saves and replay remain under Core authority. New attack starts retain priority over old follow-through; resolved attacks begin at contact, and terminal death keeps priority. Pause freezes motion. Reduced Effects suppresses optional motion and settles the monument's containment without replay when effects return. A restored defeated breach starts settled.

Existing joints and resources are reused; shared character materials remain untouched. Exact authored IDs select the new families without replacing earlier regional or player motion. Co-op shares the base rigs and anticipation; event-driven clips are verified through the solo production adapter.
